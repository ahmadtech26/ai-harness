## Jordan: The Premier Destination for Citizenship Through Investment (Updated for July 2025)

Jordan stands out as an exceptional option for acquiring second citizenship through investment programs due to several unique advantages that distinguish it from other countries. With a focus on economic stability and job creation, the Jordanian Cabinet approved new regulations on **July 2, 2025**, fundamentally restructuring and diversifying the pathways for foreign investors to obtain Jordanian nationality or residency.

### Strategic Advantages of Investing in Jordan

Jordan enjoys remarkable political and economic stability, making it a secure environment for individuals seeking safe investments and a promising future for their families. The Jordanian Dinar’s peg to the US Dollar provides additional currency stability, safeguarding against fluctuation risks. The updated citizenship-by-investment programs are designed for efficiency, with processing times for citizenship potentially achievable within months. While previously cited costs varied, the new framework introduces diverse investment thresholds, including options like **JOD 1 million (approx. USD 1.41 million)** for new shares in Jordanian companies or **JOD 700,000 (approx. USD 987,000)** for new projects in Amman, making it accessible across a broader spectrum of investors. Jordanian citizenship continues to offer numerous benefits, including visa-free travel to over 50 countries, unrestricted real estate ownership (with new rules focusing on developer purchases), a distinguished education system, high-quality healthcare, and an attractive business environment in a strategic location.

### Diverse Pathways to Jordanian Citizenship and Residency

The refreshed Jordanian CBI program introduces several distinct routes reflecting the government’s emphasis on direct economic contribution and job creation. Key pathways include:

- **Company Shares:** Purchasing at least **JOD 1 million** in new shares of Jordanian companies, to be held for three years, with no more than 20% in a single company.
- **New Investment Projects:** Establishing a new productive-sector project with minimum capital of **JOD 700,000** in Amman (or **JOD 500,000** outside the capital), which must create **20** (or **10** outside Amman) verified Jordanian jobs.
- **Existing Projects:** Acquiring new shares in existing projects worth **JOD 1 million or more**, provided at least **JOD 500,000** is invested in fixed assets and **20 new jobs** are created. Existing investors can also qualify if their average share of fixed assets is **JOD 700,000** (or **JOD 350,000** outside Amman) over three years, maintaining specific employment levels.
- **Employment-Based Qualification:** A novel route that grants citizenship to investors who employ **150** Jordanians in Amman (or **100** outside Amman), registered with the Social Security Corporation for at least a year, with employment sustained for two years post-naturalization.
- **Central Bank Deposit/Treasury Bonds:** While specific new details for this route were not fully detailed in recent announcements, previous options included a **USD 1,000,000** deposit or a **USD 1,000,000** investment in treasury bonds. It’s advisable to confirm the latest combined requirements for this specific path.

For those seeking residency without direct citizenship, a **five-year residency permit** can be obtained by purchasing a property from **developers** (rather than existing owners) valued at a minimum of **JOD 200,000**, to be held for at least five years. The program also facilitates family inclusion, extending citizenship to spouses, dependent unmarried daughters, and unmarried sons under 24. For investments exceeding **JOD 2 million**, male children up to 30 years old, their spouses, and children may also qualify. A new annual cap of **500 investors** for citizenship applications has been set.
