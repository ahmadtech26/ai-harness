## Jordan Opens Doors to Investors: Facilitated Citizenship and Unprecedented Investment Benefits! (Updated for July 2025)

The Jordanian Cabinet has approved significant new amendments to the regulations for granting Jordanian citizenship and residency to investors, effective **July 2, 2025**.These strategic changes aim to substantially enhance Jordan’s attractiveness for foreign investment by facilitating citizenship conditions and providing more direct and impactful benefits to investors and their families.The new framework prioritizes active economic contribution, particularly job creation, over passive investment, while introducing greater flexibility and transparency.

### Jordan’s Investment Citizenship Program: Gateway to a Brighter Future

Jordan’s revamped investment citizenship program is a golden opportunity for investors seeking a second nationality and a secure environment for their investments.The program now offers **eight distinct investment pathways** tailored to diverse investor interests and financial capabilities, making it one of the most flexible and impactful programs in the region.Importantly, the **passive bank deposit and treasury bond options have been entirely eliminated from the new framework for citizenship**, signaling a clear focus on direct economic engagement.

**Available Investment Options for Citizenship now include:**

- **New Shares in Jordanian Companies:** Purchasing at least **JOD 1 million (approx. USD 1.41 million)** in new shares of Jordanian companies, held for three years, with a limit of 20% concentration in any single company. The purchase must occur within four months of approval.
- **New Productive Projects:** Establishing a new project in an approved productive economic sector with a minimum paid-up capital of **JOD 700,000** in Amman (or **JOD 500,000** outside Amman), creating **20** (or **10** outside Amman) verified Jordanian jobs.
- **Acquiring Shares in Existing Productive Projects:** Purchasing new shares in an existing productive project worth at least **JOD 1 million** in paid-up capital, with at least **JOD 500,000** invested in new fixed tangible assets, creating **20 new Jordanian jobs**.
- **Existing Investments (Partners/Owners):** Qualifying if the average share of fixed and tangible non-current assets over the last three audited years is at least **JOD 700,000** in Amman (or **JOD 350,000** outside), while maintaining specific job levels (90% of 20 jobs in Amman, 10 outside).
- **High Employment Generation:** A new, direct pathway for investors who employ **150** Jordanian citizens in Amman (or **100** outside Amman), provided they are registered with Social Security for a year and employment is maintained for two years post-citizenship.
- **Sector-Specific Investments:** Designated commercial sectors like pharmaceutical/medical device warehouses and large-scale food storage have specific thresholds (e.g., minimum **JOD 3 million** company capital) and job requirements (e.g., 20/10 Jordanian employees).

For those seeking residency without full citizenship, a five-year residency permit is now available by purchasing real estate from licensed developers (not existing owners) with a minimum value of JOD 200,000, held for at least five years.

### Unprecedented Benefits and Confidentiality

Jordan’s updated citizenship program goes beyond just a passport, offering a wide range of benefits that enhance the quality of life and open new horizons for investors and their families. The Jordanian passport provides visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to over 50 destinations globally, facilitating business and tourism. Family reunification is a key feature, allowing investors to sponsor their wives, dependent parents, unmarried daughters, widowed and divorced daughters under their care, and unmarried sons under **24 years old**.Significantly, for investments exceeding **JOD 2 million**, male children up to **30 years old**, along with their spouses and children, are now also eligible for citizenship.All application procedures are conducted with complete confidentiality, ensuring the privacy of applicants and the security of their investments, with the government emphasizing support for national industries and a new annual cap of 500 investors for citizenship. Jordan’s diverse and growing economy, coupled with its commitment to unrestricted capital movement and robust banking facilities, solidifies its position as a premier destination for secure and impactful investment.
