### Jordanian Citizenship by Investment

The Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan offers a structured Citizenship by Investment (CBI) program designed to attract foreign capital and talent, providing a secure pathway for investors and their families to gain Jordanian citizenship or five-year residency. Governed by **Cabinet Decision No. (3657) of 2021** and subsequent updates, notably **Cabinet Decision No. (6404) of 2022**, the program aims to stimulate vital economic sectors. Beyond its political stability and strategic location at the crossroads of three continents, Jordan offers a safe haven for wealth preservation, access to quality healthcare and education, and an enriched quality of life within a vibrant cultural heritage.

To qualify for Jordanian citizenship, investors have several distinct financial pathways. One option involves a **USD 1,000,000** commitment: either as a three-year, interest-free deposit at the Central Bank (non-breakable/non-borrowable), or through a six-year investment in treasury bonds with interest determined by the Central Bank (non-disposable). Alternatively, an investor can purchase shares or stakes worth at least **USD 1,500,000** in productive Jordanian SMEs, which must be held for a minimum of three years with a lien. All paths require rigorous security approvals and a demonstration of financial solvency.

Another significant route focuses on job creation through new or existing investment projects. For **new projects**, establishing a business with a paid-up capital of at least **USD 1,000,000** in Amman (or **USD 750,000** outside Amman) and creating **20** (or **10** outside Amman) real job opportunities for Jordanians qualifies. For **existing investments**, the investor’s average share of fixed assets must be at least **USD 1,000,000** in Amman (or **USD 500,000** outside Amman) over three years, while maintaining **20** (or **10** outside Amman) Jordanian jobs. Additionally, a new provision allows for citizenship by employing a significant number of Jordanians: **150** in Amman or **100** in other governorates, provided they have been registered with social security for a year and are retained for two years post-citizenship.

The program extends benefits to the investor’s immediate family, including spouse, unmarried/widowed/divorced daughters under their care, and male children under 18 years. Notably, for investments exceeding **USD 3 million**, citizenship can also be granted to male children up to 30 years old, along with their wives and children, broadening the scope of intergenerational security. Furthermore, a separate **five-year residency** option is available for those purchasing real estate valued at a minimum of **JOD 200,000**, which must be held for at least five years for residential purposes.

In essence, Jordan’s Citizenship by Investment program offers a robust framework for global investors seeking enhanced mobility and a stable environment. With its flexible investment options across diverse sectors, including industry, agriculture, tourism, ICT, education, health, media, and specific commercial ventures, the program is tailored to support both the Kingdom’s economic growth and the personal and financial security of its new citizens. The comprehensive nature of the program, coupled with strong government guarantees and a commitment to confidentiality, positions Jordan as a highly attractive destination for discerning investors in 2025.
