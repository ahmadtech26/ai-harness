# **Apartment Rent Contract form**

**Lessor:**————————————.

**Lessee:**__________________________

**The amount of rent:** ———– Jordan Dinar Shall be paid in Advance at the beginning of every month.

**Lease start date:**Sep 1,2021

**Rental duration:**One year

**Usage of the rental property:**House to live

**Rental description:**Apartment number ———- from the building on the land plot number ———- Amman city.

Since the lessor owns the property described above and since the Lessee wishes to rent it, the two parties
## **have agreed on the following:**

**First**: The introduction to this contract, its conditions, and appendices, if any, are considered an integral part of it, and are read with it as a one unit.

**Second**: The Lessee acknowledges that he has received the leased property and its accessories safe from any defect and has personally inspected all its contents.

**Third**: The Lessee must, before the expiry of the contract period, if he does not wish to renew for a similar period, inform the lessor at least two months before the expiry of the contract period, otherwise shall renewed for another similar period if the Lessor wants to do so.

**Fourth**: The Lessee is obligated to pay all fees, expenses and bills imposed on the property, including guard fees, cleaning, electricity, telephone, property tax, and Maaref tax, in addition to all maintenance expenses.

**Fifth**: If the lessee refuses or delays the payment of any of the installments of the rent allowance after the lapse of ten days from its due date, then all installments of the contract become payable immediately and in one payment.

**Sixth**: If the lessor wishes to terminate the contract at the end of its term or does not wish to renew the contract for a similar period, he is exempted from giving the warning required by the Lessors and Lessees Law, and he may submit an urgent request to terminate the contract immediately after the expiry of the period specified by the law.

**Seventh**: If the contract term expires and the lessee fails to hand over the leased apartment and its key, does not pay a new lease for the subsequent period, or does not sign a new lease contract after the expiry of this contract, the lessor may, after the expiry of the term of this contract, choose one of the following two options:

**The first option:** to open the door of the apartment in any way, including breaking the doors, which shall be in the presence of one witness or the building guard, and The lessor shall write  report about the belongings of the lessee which exists inside the apartment,  then he shall keep it for a period of one week in the building’s warehouse or any other place. If the Lessee does not attend to receive his belongings within this period, the lesser may also throw it in the garbage in the presence of any witness. All this is at the expense of the Lessee and at least five hundred dinars are owed to the Lessee instead of opening the door in this way.

The second option: for the Lessor to keep the property closed as it is until the Lessee delivers the key to the leased property, with a cumulative progressive increase of 25% for each calendar month of the monthly rent value starting from the day following the end of the first contract.
