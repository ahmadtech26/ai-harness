# Foreign non-profit organization, representative power of Attorney form

**Power of Attorney**

**Foreign Non-Profit Organization (Representative Office)**

This Power of Attorney is made this _______ day of _______**, 20**, by [Organization Name], an organization duly organized and existing under and by virtue of the laws of [Country Name], whose registered office is at [Address] (hereinafter referred to as the “Organization”).

This deed witnesses that the above-mentioned Organization hereby nominates, constitutes, and appoints [Representative Name(s)] to be its true and lawful representative(s) in the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan, and for it and in its name and on its behalf, to do or perform all or any of the following acts and things:

- To register an operating foreign organization in Jordan in conformity with the Law of Societies (No. (51) of 2008); and the Law Amending the Law on Societies (No. (22) of 2009) and any amendments thereto or any other relevant legislation.
- To function as the Organization’s duly authorized representative(s) in Jordan for all intents and purposes of the Law of Societies (No. (51) of 2008); and the Law Amending the Law on Societies (No. (22) of 2009) and its amendments thereto or any other relevant legislation.
- To represent the Organization before all ministries, authorities, government departments, bodies & institutions in Jordan.
- To operate and manage the affairs of the Organization’s office in Jordan and to conduct any business thereof.
- To accept service and receive letters and notices on the Organization’s behalf.
- To sign, make, execute, file, submit and do all applications, instruments, documents, forms, declarations, acts, deeds, and things which the said representative(s) shall consider necessary or proper to operate the Organization’s affairs as an operating foreign organization in Jordan in conformity with Jordanian laws and all matters emanating therefrom.
- To sign, make, execute, file, submit and do all applications, forms, and declarations to the Ministry of Finance/Customs, Ministry of Social Development, and all other ministries and government departments in connection with obtaining the exemptions and facilities provided in the Law of Societies in force and its amendments or any other relevant legislation and to clear from customs, take out and receive any goods coming into Jordan in the Organization’s name for the purpose of benefiting from the exemptions stipulated in the Law of Societies in force.
- To represent the Organization before banks, opening and closing accounts, depositing, or withdrawing from these accounts. Generally, to function as the Organization’s duly authorized representative(s) in all financial and legal relevant matters.
- To delegate to a third party part of his/her/their previously mentioned powers (except for articles 1 & 2).

The Organization hereby ratifies, confirms, and agrees to ratify and confirm whatsoever shall be lawfully done by the said representative(s) or any substitute or substitutes as previously mentioned under or by virtue of these presents.

In witness whereof, the Organization has caused this Power of Attorney to be executed this _______ day of _______**, 20**.

**By:**
