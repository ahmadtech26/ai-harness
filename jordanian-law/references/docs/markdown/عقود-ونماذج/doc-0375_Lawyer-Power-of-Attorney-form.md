# Lawyer Power of Attorney

Jordan Lawyer form

**Power of Attorney**

I, [Client Name], hereby authorize Advocates Sami Alawad and Omar Shihadeh, jointly and severally, to represent me in all legal matters before any court in the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Filing and pursuing lawsuits and claims, including original, reciprocal, and crossbar cases.
- Defending against lawsuits and claims.
- Making objections and appeals.
- Handling matters related to judgments, including judgments in absentia.
- Claiming legal interests, fees, and expenses.
- Dealing with provisional and executive seizures.
- Representing me before Executive Service Directorates.
- Receiving, depositing, and cashing checks.
- Giving and receiving receipts, reconciliations, and clearances.
- Negotiating settlements.
- Handling matters related to imprisonment and release.
- Submitting summonses, requests, briefs, warnings, and other necessary documents.
- Receiving and responding to notifications and notices.

This Power of Attorney grants my attorneys full authority to act on my behalf in all related matters until the final resolution of any legal proceedings. They may also appoint or remove other lawyers at their discretion to assist in these matters.

This Power of Attorney is granted freely and with my full understanding and consent.

**Client Name:**
