# **Requirements for Opening a Gold Manufacturing and Forming Factory in Jordan**

Opening a gold manufacturing and forming factory in Jordan requires fulfilling a set of conditions and requirements, covering legal, technical, and financial aspects. Here is a summary of the most important conditions:

- **Legal Aspects:**

- **Company Registration:** The factory must be registered as a formal company with the Ministry of Industry, Trade, and Supply. This requires submitting a set of documents, such as the company’s articles of incorporation, bylaws, and copies of shareholders’ identification cards.
- **Obtaining Necessary Licenses:** Operating the factory requires obtaining several licenses, including:

  - Professional license from the relevant municipality.
  - Approvals from public safety and environmental authorities.
  - Licenses from security authorities, given the nature of the materials the factory handles.
- **Compliance with Laws and Regulations:** The factory must comply with all laws and regulations related to gold manufacturing and trading in Jordan, including consumer protection laws and approved standards and specifications.

- **Technical Aspects:**

- **Providing a Suitable Location:** A suitable location for the factory must be chosen, providing the necessary security and ease of access, while observing safety and environmental requirements.
- **Equipping the Factory with Necessary Equipment:** The factory must be equipped with the necessary machinery and equipment for gold manufacturing and forming, including casting and forming machines, cutting and engraving devices, and inspection and quality equipment.
- **Employing Trained Technical Labor:** Trained technical labor with the necessary skills and experience in gold manufacturing and forming must be employed.
- **Securing Raw Materials:** Reliable sources for supplying raw gold must be secured, while adhering to approved standards and specifications.

- **Financial Aspects:**

- **Providing Necessary Capital:** Establishing a gold manufacturing and forming factory requires providing a large amount of capital to cover construction, equipment, and operating costs.
- **Preparing an Economic Feasibility Study:** A detailed economic feasibility study of the project must be prepared to assess the financial and economic viability of the factory.
- **Additional procedures**
- It may be necessary to obtain approvals from other entities such as the Ministry of the Interior.
- Commitment to the conditions and standards of “Made in Jordan” if there is a desire to use this logo.

**Useful resources**

- Ministry of interior.

**Note:** Requirements and conditions may change over time, so it is advisable to review the relevant official authorities for the latest information.
