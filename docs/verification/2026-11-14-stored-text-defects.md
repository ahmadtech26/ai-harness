## 5. Repealed articles that serve «ملغاة» and the whole repealed text (191)

the word «ملغاة» (the model could quote it as law in force). For commerce, the Gazette shows the repeal while the Bureau
still prints the old text (the records are `differs` / `bureau` / `repeal`); for penal-code 124–126 it is the Bureau that
shows the repeal, and the repealing issue is offline (`unverifiable`). Neither compares the wording. Each commerce
article also still ends with the next article's heading (§1, «no sentence end to cut at»). A fix keeps «ملغاة» alone,
from the repealing law's Gazette text, one law per PR.
