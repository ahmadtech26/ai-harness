#### Notes for the lawyer and the owner

- **59 مكرر.** The text in force is composed from 20/2005 art. 2 (Gazette 4720 pp. 3926–3928, the whole article) and 31/2017 art. 13 (5479 pp. 5397–5398). Art. 13 خامساً reads «بإلغاء الفقرة (٤) منها وبإعادة ترقيم الفقرتين (٥) و(٦) الواردتين فيها لتصبحا (٦) و(٧) منها وبإضافة الفقرة (٥)». I read the repealed (4) as the *original* (4), the absence rule, which the new (5) replaces («تطبق أحكام المادة (٦٧) … على حضور وغياب أطراف الدعوى»). The original (3) became (4) under رابعاً and stays. The corpus has the same paragraphs. **Lawyer to confirm.** The corpus lacks the paragraph numbers 4–9 and has «وحصر» for «و- حصر». The 2005 passages are printed without hamza on many alifs («ادارة», «الاشراف», «احكام», «او»), where the corpus writes them with hamza. **Owner:** the missing paragraph numbers 4–9 and the item letter «و-» are label differences, not word differences, so no fix PR adds them (the coordinator's rule: fix PRs change only word differences, not hamza or label style). They keep 59 مكرر `differs` after any fix.
- **188 مكرر(1).** The Gazette (5479 p. 5409, read again at high zoom) prints «لإلقاء الحجز الاحتياطي», as the corpus and the Bureau do; «لإلغاء» (cancelling the attachment) is what the sense needs. A slip in the Gazette itself, so the corpus stays as printed and nothing is fixed. **Lawyer:** note it; an erratum (استدراك) in a later issue was not looked for.
- **11, 14, 64, 81 مكرر, 121.** The Gazette prints «او», «الالكتروني», «احضار», «اجازت», «الاثبات», «اعضاء», «اثباتها», «الاصلية» and «امكن» without hamza, where the corpus writes hamza. 84 and 86 are the other way round: the Gazette has «إذا» and «إلى».

Its set-aside entry now quotes the stored words. **109.** The stored first line «الدفوع الشكلية في الدعوى المدنية» (the copy site's title) is set aside like 83's «إجراءات الكشف والخبرة».
